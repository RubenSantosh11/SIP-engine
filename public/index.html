    How the engine works

    1. Collect public reviews and comments: Groww and seven other Indian investing apps (Play Store and App Store), YouTube comments on SIP videos, TradingQ&A and ValuePickr forums. Usernames are hashed.
    2. Filter: a free keyword check keeps text mentioning SIPs; a model then judges relevance in batches of 10.
    3. Discover themes: the model open-codes 100 records in its own words; codes are clustered into a draft codebook, which the PM curated (merges, renames, scope changes are logged).
    4. Extract: every relevant record is coded one at a time against the codebook. Every value must carry an exact quote; code checks each quote against the source and drops any that don't match.
    5. Score in plain Python: reach (Wilson interval), severity, stop-lift (risk ratio of people who stopped vs people still investing, Katz interval), addressability; ranked by the lower confidence bound, and only with 30+ records from 2+ sources.
    6. Validate against a hand-labelled golden set, and log every place the PM corrected the AI.

    The model never counts, ranks or summarises. It fills a typed schema for one record at a time.
