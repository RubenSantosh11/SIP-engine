## My working framework (for Part 2)
12-month continuation = setup success (first instalment + autopay active) -> no involuntary lapse (each debit succeeds: mandate health, balance on debit day, bank changes, app/payment errors, reminders) -> no voluntary stop (expectations vs what they see in months 1-6, ability to reduce/skip/pause instead of cancel, cash shocks, confusion, outside advice) -> recovery (restart within 30 days after a lapse). Under the "successful instalment in last 30 days" definition, reducing keeps a user active; skip/pause risks a 30-day gap but avoids losing them.
