# Interviews: first-time SIP investors on Groww (Part 3)

## Who to recruit (5-6 people)
- Started their **first** mutual-fund SIP on **Groww** 2-12 months ago (a few up to 18 months is fine).
- At least **2-3 who have paused, missed, reduced or thought about stopping**; 1-2 who are steadily continuing, for contrast.
- Mix if you can: salaried vs irregular income; different ages; started on their own vs because someone suggested it.

## Screener (send on WhatsApp)
> Hi! I'm doing a short college research project on how people find investing through SIPs in their first year. It's a 20-25 min call, nothing to prepare, and I won't ask about amounts, account details or screenshots. Could you answer 4 quick questions?
> 1. When did you start your first mutual fund SIP? (month/year)
> 2. Which app do you use for it?
> 3. Since starting, have you ever paused, skipped, reduced, missed or thought about stopping it? (yes / no / not sure)
> 4. Free for a 20-min call today or tomorrow? Which time works?

Pick people who say Groww and started within ~12-18 months. Prioritise "yes" or "not sure" on Q3.

## Before you start (1 minute, read aloud)
- "This is for a college project, not for Groww or any company. There are no right answers."
- "I won't ask for account numbers, PAN, amounts or screenshots. Please don't share them."
- "Is it OK if I take notes? I'll only use them anonymously." (Get a yes.)

## Questions (about 20 minutes; skip what's already answered)
**1. Starting (3 min)**
- Tell me about when you started your first SIP. What made you start then?
- Who or what influenced you? (friend, family, YouTube, colleague, app ad)
- What did you expect to happen in the first year? What would "it's working" look like?

**2. Since starting (5 min)**
- Walk me through what's happened since. Anything surprise you?
- When do you open the app now? What do you look at first? How often?
- How do you feel when you see the number? Can you remember a specific time it felt bad or good?

**3. Pausing / stopping (5 min), the key section**
- Have you ever paused, skipped, reduced, missed an instalment, or thought about stopping?
  - If yes: take me back to that moment. What was going on? What did you do in the app? What happened next?
  - Was it something you decided, or did it just happen (payment failed, forgot, low balance)?
  - If no: has there been a moment you came close? What kept you going?
- If an instalment failed or was missed: how did you find out? What did you do?

**4. When unsure (3 min)**
- When you're unsure about your SIP, what do you do? (Google, YouTube, ask someone, ignore it, check the app)
- Is there anything in the app you've never quite understood?

**5. What would help (3 min)**
- If you could change one thing about how the SIP part of the app works, what would it be?
- What would make you more confident about continuing for the next year?

**Close:** "Thank you! Could I message you on Saturday to try a small prototype for 10-15 minutes?" (You need 3 testers for Part 6.)

## Rules
- Ask about **past behaviour and specific moments**, not opinions ("tell me about the last time…", not "would you…").
- Don't suggest answers or mention our findings (payment failures, etc.) before they bring it up.
- Never give investment advice or mention funds.
