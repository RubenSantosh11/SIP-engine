How the scores work

Reach
Share of records with a stated or clear SIP status that mention the theme through a verified quote.
Severity
Mean severity of mentions: 1 minor, 2 clear reason or friction, 3 decisive or strong distress.
Stop-lift
How much more often people who stopped, paused or reduced mention the theme than people still investing (a risk ratio, Katz 95% interval). People who only moved their SIP to another app are not counted as stopping.
Addressability
From the codebook: high —, medium —, low —.
Coding
Each record is coded on its own against a fixed codebook, with a verbatim quote for every coded value; code checks each quote against the source and drops any that do not match. All counting and scoring is plain Python.

4. Limitations

  • Reddit, X, Instagram and Quora were not used: no permitted access.
  • Reviews and forum posts are not a random sample. App-store reviews lean towards complaints, so product friction is over-represented next to reasons such as returns or cash needs.
  • People who stopped come mostly from app-store reviews and people still investing mostly from YouTube, so stop-lift partly reflects where a theme is discussed. Read it as directional.
  • SIP status, first-time status and time since starting are self-reported and rarely stated, so the first-year view is descriptive only.
  • Most reviews are about apps other than Groww. The findings describe first-time SIP investing in India in general.
  • Coding was done by Claude inside a Claude Code session, not through an API; the same model wrote the prompts. The hand-labelled golden set is the independent check, and it is small.
  • Quotes were checked against their sources before submission as well as by the pipeline, so the verbatim pass rate reflects that self-check.
  • The second pass only coded posts with a cue word, so signals phrased differently are missed.
